# Output Contract

Use the smallest deliverable that satisfies the request. When the user does not specify one, return the full package below.

## 0. Delivery brief and timing budget

Before drafting, record the audience, language, duration, slide count, speaker level, requested files, and visual preference. When duration is fixed, choose an expected `语速` and reserve time for questions, pauses, slide changes, and breathing before setting the target `词数`. Do not write a long script and estimate the time afterward.

For revised work, also record the source hierarchy: `用户新增要求`, `核心材料`, and `辅助材料`. The newest explicit user request controls format and delivery; the named core source controls the argument; supporting files preserve useful context and continuity.

## 1. Source-grounded content map

Include:

- topic and intended audience;
- one central question;
- one theme anchor of no more than two sentences;
- 3–7 core claims and their relationships;
- evidence ledger using `材料事实`, `讲者观点`, `合理推演`, and `待核实`;
- stories, examples, analogies, memorable source phrases, and material gaps.

For long material, map the beginning, middle, and end before compressing repetition. Do not summarize only the opening chapters.

## 2. One-page summary

Write:

1. **一句话结论**
2. **内容主线**
3. **关键论点**
4. **论证链条**
5. **值得保留的故事与类比**
6. **对听众的启示**
7. **待核实内容**

## 3. Paste-ready slide copy

For every slide provide the following slots. Wording in `上屏文字` must be ready to paste into a deck.

```markdown
### 第 N 页｜<页面角色>

- 标题：<短而有结论的标题>
- 副标题：<可选>
- 本页结论：<一句完整判断>
- 上屏文字：
  - <精炼句 1>
  - <精炼句 2>
  - <精炼句 3>
- 关键词：<2–5 个>
- 强调元素：<数字、短引语或对比；没有则省略>
- 图示节点文字：<图中真正显示的词；没有图则省略>
- 故事／类比钩子：<可选>
- 内容提问：<可选，但整场至少三类问题；讲稿必须明确说出本页问题或等义问题>
- 主题回扣：<本页如何推进主题锚点；不需要时省略>
- 视觉建议：<图、流程、时间线、对比或留白>
```

Prefer conclusion-led titles. Avoid empty labels such as “背景介绍”, “相关分析”, “几点思考”, or “未来展望” when a concrete claim is available.

Keep one dominant judgment per slide. Default to 1–4 short text groups, steps, labels, or diagram nodes rather than a paragraph. `上屏文字` must contain the exact words the audience will see, including button-like labels, timelines, comparison terms, and question text.

## 4. Page-aligned speech notes

After each slide, write speech notes with these functions in natural prose:

- entry or transition;
- explanation beyond the screen;
- story, analogy, or example when it improves understanding;
- evidence boundary when a claim is opinion, inference, or uncertain;
- one audible language marker;
- the slide's content question, spoken explicitly or in equivalent wording, followed by a brief pause or answer;
- exit sentence that leads to the next page.

Do not read the bullets verbatim. A listener should understand the concept with the screen hidden; a viewer should understand the structure with the audio muted.

Every section must be visibly tied to its PPT page number. When speaker notes exist inside a PPTX, their final wording must match the corresponding section of the external speech script.

## 5. Required opening

If the user provides a greeting, group identity, course context, or thanks to the teacher, keep those lines first. The opening contract then uses:

> 我保证，不是一个笑话。

Then complete four moves: connect to the listener, ask the central question, introduce the theme anchor, and promise a concrete gain. When no greeting or thanks is required, the promise may be the first spoken sentence. If the user requests a different opening, follow that request.

## 6. Required close

Close in this order:

1. `感谢`：感谢时间与注意力；
2. `致敬`：为认真学习、理解复杂问题的行动致敬；
3. `获得`：点明听众获得的框架、地图、判断工具、表达方式或行动线索；
4. `期待`：期待听众未来如何观察、验证、表达或行动。

Tie the close back to the opening question and theme anchor. Do not end with a generic “谢谢大家”.

## 7. Quality note

End the written deliverable with a compact note listing transcription uncertainties, compressed sections, unsupported source claims, and any external verification still needed.

## 8. Revision synchronization and file delivery

When the speech changes, update every dependent artifact in the same pass:

- PPT 演讲者备注;
- page-aligned speech script;
- 中文译文 or other requested translation;
- per-slide and total timing guidance;
- usage or rehearsal instructions;
- recurring theme wording and closing promise.

Shorten in this order: remove duplicated meaning, shorten transitions and examples, replace complex wording, merge secondary support, then consider removing a core claim. Report the final word count and estimated delivery time.

When actual files are requested, complete `逐页渲染`: render and inspect every PPT slide and every DOCX page. Verify page-to-slide `对应`, text fit, readable type, compact but usable document layout, package integrity, and the absence of clipping or overflow. Do not claim completion from successful generation alone.
